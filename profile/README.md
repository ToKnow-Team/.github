### 🔭 What We Are Building

We are building an ecosystem for online tutors that brings together the tools they use every day:

- smart collaborative whiteboard
- personal workspace for tutors and students
- lesson and student management tools
- analytics and productivity features

Our goal is to make online teaching more convenient, structured, and efficient.
